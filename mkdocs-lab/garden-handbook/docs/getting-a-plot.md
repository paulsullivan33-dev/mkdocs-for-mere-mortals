# Getting a Plot

Plots are let for one season at a
time, from the first working party in
March to the tidy-up day in November.
This page explains how the waiting
list works and what a plot costs.

## The waiting list

Write your name on the list at the
spring meeting, or ask any committee
member at any time of year. When a
plot comes free, it is offered to the
top of the list. You have two weeks to
say yes before the offer passes to the
next name.

!!! warning "One plot per household"
    Plots are in short supply. One
    plot per household, no exceptions,
    until everyone on the list has
    been offered one.

## Fees

| Plot size        | Fee per season |
| ---------------- | -------------: |
| Full plot (5 × 4 m) |         £30 |
| Half plot        |         £18 |
| Raised bed       |         £10 |

The fee covers water, the shared tool
store, and the autumn delivery of
manure. It is payable at the spring
meeting or within a month of taking
the plot.

## What you sign up to

- Keep the plot reasonably weeded —
  your neighbours' seeds do not care
  whose side of the path they land
  on, but your neighbours do.
- Work one communal job per season:
  path mowing, hedge cutting, or the
  compost turn. The rota is in
  [Meetings and Jobs](meetings-and-jobs.md).
- Nothing taller than a person at the
  south edge, so you do not shade the
  plot behind you.
