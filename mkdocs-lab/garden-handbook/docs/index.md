# Maple Street Community Garden — Members' Handbook

Welcome to the garden. This handbook is
the collected knowledge of the Maple
Street Community Garden: how to get a
plot, how to look after it, what to do
with the compost heap, and how the
garden runs itself.

It is a *fictional* garden, grown for
this guide as an example documentation
site. Everything in it is the sort of
thing a real garden, club or small
project writes down once it gets tired
of answering the same questions twice.

## Start here

- New to the garden? [Getting a
  Plot](getting-a-plot.md) explains
  the waiting list and the plot fee.
- Already have a plot? [Looking After
  Your Plot](plot-care.md) covers
  watering, weeding and the rules
  everyone agreed to.
- Wondering what may go on the heap?
  [Compost](compost.md) has the list.

## The three rules nobody argues about

1. Lock the gate when you leave.
2. Water is shared: fill your cans,
   then coil the hose for the next
   person.
3. If you borrow a tool, clean it and
   put it back the same day.

!!! note "This handbook is the record"
    Decisions made at meetings are
    written up in
    [Meetings and Jobs](meetings-and-jobs.md).
    If it isn't written here, it was
    probably never decided.
