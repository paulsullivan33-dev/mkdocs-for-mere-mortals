# Meetings and Jobs

The garden runs on one meeting a
month, March to November, in the
village hall or — in summer — at the
picnic bench by the gate.

## The spring jobs list

Every season starts with the same
list. Tick them off as the working
party gets through them:

- [x] Order the manure delivery
- [x] Pump the barrow tyres
- [ ] Turn the compost heap
- [ ] Clear the ditch along the lane
- [ ] Re-paint the plot numbers
- [ ] Check the standpipe for leaks

Committee posts are re-elected every
March. Any plot holder can stand, and
nobody is allowed to hold the same
post for more than three years in a
row — a rule the garden is proud of,
and the reason this handbook keeps
getting new editors.
